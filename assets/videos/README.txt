Hero background video goes here.

Save it as:  hero.mp4  (and optionally hero.webm)
Recommended: 1920x1080 or 1280x720, 10–20 seconds, no audio, under ~6 MB.

The site looks for assets/videos/hero.webm and assets/videos/hero.mp4 (see SITE.hero.videos
in js/main.js). If neither file exists, or the visitor has reduced-motion / data-saver on,
the cinematic photo slideshow plays instead — nothing breaks.

Free, licence-friendly sources to search: Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Coverr
(search "paneer", "tandoor", "naan", "Indian sweets", "Indian kitchen").
Best of all: a 15-second phone clip of your own tandoor and sweets counter.
