Our Story

More than food. It's an experience.

At Shreya Sweets & Restaurants, every table is set for the people who matter most — families, friends and travellers passing through Bhadohi.

Ours is a vegetarian kitchen, bringing together the comfort of North Indian gravies, breads from the tandoor and a counter of sweets made with desi ghee and khoya. Whether you sit down for a family meal or stop by for a box of mithai, you'll find the same warmth in every plate.

Come hungry, stay a while, and let us take care of the rest.
